Features

A deeper look at the building blocks.

These are common capabilities, not a checklist you need to buy. We choose the pieces that make sense for the workflow.

01

Interactions

Slash commands, context menus, buttons, select menus, modals and guided multi-step flows.

02

Roles

Automatic role assignment, gated areas, staff permissions and role-based controls.

03

Tickets

Private support channels, intake forms, routing, transcripts and closure workflows.

04

Applications

Structured forms, reviewer queues, status updates and approval flows.

05

Moderation

Warnings, reports, filters, logs, evidence capture and appeals.

06

Data

Persistent settings, member records, activity history and structured project data.

07

Notifications

Webhooks, scheduled messages, alerts and event-driven updates.

08

Integrations

External APIs, websites, forms, spreadsheets and other services where appropriate.

09

Analytics

Useful summaries and operational metrics rather than vanity numbers.

10

Admin tools

Staff-only commands, configuration flows and lightweight control panels.

11

Scheduled jobs

Reminders, recurring reports, clean-up jobs and time-based workflows.

12

Deployment

Environment configuration, launch support, logs and sensible operational documentation.

The important part

A feature only belongs in the build if it improves the actual workflow. We would rather ship twelve useful pieces than thirty impressive-sounding ones nobody uses.
