What “custom” means here

Your server is the brief.

01

No forced feature list

We do not start by asking you to pick from a menu of commands. We start with the problem and decide what actually needs to exist.

02

Designed for your staff

Commands, permissions and logs are planned around the people who will operate the system every day.

03

Built to grow

A small first version can leave room for a dashboard, database, integrations or new workflows later without throwing everything away.
