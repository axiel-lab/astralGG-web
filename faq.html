FAQ

The questions people usually ask before starting.

If your question is not here, send it anyway. A useful first conversation does not require you to know the technical vocabulary.

How much does a custom Discord bot cost?

Small focused projects can start around $120, while more involved systems often land around $300–$650+ depending on data, integrations, permissions and testing. Pricing is discussed before development begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope and how quickly feedback is available. A focused bot can be relatively quick; a multi-system project needs more design, testing and iteration. We give a realistic estimate after understanding the workflow.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You can describe what should happen in plain language. Technical decisions are part of our job.

Can you work with an existing bot?

Yes, when the existing project and codebase are accessible and in reasonable shape. Sometimes extending an existing system is the right answer; sometimes rebuilding one small area is safer.

Can the bot use a database?

Yes. Persistent data is common for applications, tickets, settings, reputation, schedules and other workflows.

Can you connect Discord to a website or API?

Yes. Webhooks and APIs can be useful when they remove duplicate work or bring information into the right place.

Who owns the code?

Ownership and delivery terms should be clear before work starts. We can discuss the exact arrangement for your project during the quote.

Do you provide hosting?

Hosting can be handled on your infrastructure or, when appropriate, as part of an ongoing arrangement. See the hosting page for the general approach.

Can I start with a small version?

Absolutely. A deliberately small first release is often the smartest way to prove the workflow before adding advanced features.

Do you fix bugs after launch?

Yes. The support arrangement depends on the project, but post-launch fixes and maintenance can be discussed as part of the handoff or an ongoing plan.

Still unsure?

Send the rough version. We can work from there.
