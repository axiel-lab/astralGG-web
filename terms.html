Terms

Simple terms for a custom software studio.

Project-specific agreements always take priority where applicable. These general points describe the working relationship at a high level.

01

Scope

Work is based on the agreed project scope. New requirements can change the price or timeline when they add meaningful complexity.

02

Payment

Payment milestones, deposits and final balances are agreed before work begins. Work outside the agreed scope may require a revised quote.

03

Client responsibilities

Clients provide timely access, credentials, content and feedback where those items are required for the project. Credentials should be shared through appropriate secure methods.

04

Third-party rules

Projects must follow Discord’s rules and the terms of connected services. We will not knowingly build features intended to abuse a platform or its users.
