A small example of the interaction style.

This is a visual sample, not a live production bot. It shows the kind of concise, structured responses we aim for.

Astral Demo / #community● interactive concept
Hi! I can help you with onboarding, tickets, events or community settings. What would you like to do?
I’m new here. Where do I start?
Start with #welcome. Choose your interests there and I’ll suggest the channels and roles that fit. If you need help, you can open a private ticket from the same message.
That feels better than reading ten channels.
That’s the idea. Good automation should make the next step obvious.
Design principle

Short messages. Clear choices. Useful outcomes.

The interface of a bot matters. People should know what happened, what they can do next and where their information went.
