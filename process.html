Astral / Process
Process

A clear process, without the agency theatre.

You do not need a 40-page specification to start. We use a straightforward sequence that keeps decisions visible and prevents surprises.

01 / DISCOVER

Tell us the problem

We ask what the server does today, what is frustrating and what a better version should feel like.

02 / SCOPE

Shape the build

We turn the idea into features, boundaries, assumptions and a practical delivery plan.

03 / BUILD

Develop in layers

Core workflows come first, followed by edge cases, permissions, polish and integrations.

04 / TEST

Try the real thing

You test the bot in the environment where it will actually live and give us notes.

05 / LAUNCH

Hand over cleanly

We deploy, explain the important pieces and leave you with a project that makes sense.

What you receive

Not just a ZIP file.

01

Working software

The agreed features, tested in the context of your server.

02

Configuration guidance

Important environment variables, permissions and setup steps explained clearly.

03

A sensible handoff

You should understand how to operate the system even if you are not a developer.

Good projects are collaborative

Your feedback is part of the build.

We would rather adjust a small detail while the project is in motion than discover after launch that the workflow was misunderstood.
