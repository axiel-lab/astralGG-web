What changes the quote?

Complexity is more useful than a page count.

A five-page bot can be harder than a fifteen-command bot if it has complicated permissions, external services or edge cases.
FactorSimpleModerateComplex
Commands & interactionsFewSeveralMany connected flows
DataLightDatabaseRich relationships & history
IntegrationsNone / webhookOne or two APIsMultiple external systems
