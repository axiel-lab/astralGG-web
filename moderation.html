Moderation

Give your staff better tools for difficult moments.

Moderation is where consistency matters. Astral can build the workflows around reports, evidence, warnings, tickets, appeals and incident records so staff spend less time hunting for context.

Moderation

A calmer moderation workflow

The goal is not more automation for its own sake. It is fewer missed details and clearer decisions.
01

Reports & tickets

Give members a clear way to report problems and give staff a structured place to work through them.

02

Warnings & actions

Standardise common actions while preserving room for staff judgement when a situation is unusual.

03

Evidence & logs

Keep relevant context together so a decision can be reviewed without searching across channels.

Moderation

Staff experience

A moderation system is only good if moderators actually want to use it.
01

Permission-aware tools

Make dangerous or sensitive controls available only to the people who should have them.

02

Private workflows

Keep internal discussions, evidence and appeals out of public channels.

03

Metrics without obsession

Surface useful patterns — volume, response time, repeat issues — without turning community care into a leaderboard.

If you can explain the workflow in plain English, we can usually turn it into a clear technical plan.
