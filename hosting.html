Astral / Hosting
Hosting

Keep the bot online — and know what is happening when it isn’t.

A working bot is not finished when the code is written. Hosting, environment variables, logs, restarts and backups are part of the product too.

01 / DEPLOY

Reliable deployment

We can help place your bot on appropriate infrastructure and set up the basic runtime configuration it needs to start cleanly.

02 / OBSERVE

Logs & health

Useful logs make failures less mysterious. We structure errors and runtime information so debugging has somewhere to start.

03 / MAINTAIN

Ongoing care

For projects that need it, we can continue with fixes, updates, dependency changes and practical maintenance after launch.

Your hosting or ours

You should know who owns what.

Your infrastructure

Best when you already have a VPS or cloud account and want the project to live entirely under your ownership.

Astral-managed hosting

Useful when you want us to handle the operational layer as part of an ongoing arrangement. Exact availability and cost depend on the project.

What we avoid.

No mystery infrastructure

You should not have to guess where your bot lives or how to recover it.

No “it works on my machine” handoff

Deployment notes and environment requirements belong in the delivery conversation.
