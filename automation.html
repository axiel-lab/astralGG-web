Automations we build

Useful automations are usually simple ideas connected together carefully.
01

Member lifecycle

Welcome, verify, assign roles, schedule follow-ups and guide new members without a staff member repeating the same steps.

02

Notifications

Send the right event to the right channel or person when something changes in a website, form, database or external service.

Scheduled jobs
